    Award-Winning Texas Cook-Off Chili

    Beef Chili · Makes a 12-quart pot · About 2 to 2.5 hours

    Watch the video

    Pete's chili that took first place at a Texas state cook-off in 2006. He has posted this same pot more than once; viewers have also used it to win their own cook-offs.

    Ingredients

    • 3 lb ground beef (80/20)
    • 1 lb hot breakfast sausage (Jimmy Dean Hot)
    • 6 oz tomato paste
    • 1 large onion, chopped
    • 3 fresh jalapeños, chopped (some videos of this same recipe use 2 large jalapeños)
    • 9 cloves garlic, chopped (about 2 tbsp minced)
    • 4 oz Hatch green chiles
    • 2 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Flavor-Time Southern Seasoning (his own blend)
    • 3 oz Williams Original Chili Seasoning
    • 44 oz diced tomatoes
    • 44 oz tomato sauce
    • 3 cans (15.5 oz each) Bush's Chili Magic, Campfire Style
    • Splash of Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap (optional, to finish)

    Instructions

    1. In a 12-quart pot, brown the ground beef and drain it.
    2. Add the hot breakfast sausage (don't drain) and the tomato paste. Break everything up and cook until the sausage is fully done and the paste has cooked in.
    3. Add the onion, jalapeños, garlic, Hatch green chiles, Flavor-Time Southern Seasoning, and Williams chili seasoning. Stir and cook about 5 minutes, until the vegetables start to soften.
    4. Stir in the diced tomatoes and tomato sauce. Cover and simmer on low for at least 1 hour (Pete went 1.5 hours).
    5. Stir in the Bush's Chili Magic, cover again, and simmer another 30 minutes.
    6. Finish with a splash of Zippy Zap and serve.

    Notes

    • Flavor-Time Southern Seasoning and Zippy Zap are Pete's own products; swap in your favorite seasoning blend or hot sauce if you don't have them.
    • The written version is on page 177 of Pete's cookbook.
    • Also shown in: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/mUkmgYiTkPY and https://www.youtube.com/shorts/m68vDW-z0Bg

    Chili-Grind Chili with Beer

    Beef Chili · About 3 hours, plus browning

    Watch the video

    A bigger, beer-and-broth pot made with chili-grind beef instead of the usual ground beef and sausage mix.

    Ingredients

    • 4 lb chili-grind beef
    • 6 oz chili paste (the audio may be 'tomato paste'; 6 oz is the amount)
    • 1 large onion, chopped
    • 2 large fresh jalapeños, chopped
    • 3 oz Williams chili seasoning
    • 2 tbsp minced garlic
    • 12 oz beer
    • 28 oz diced tomatoes
    • 4 oz Hatch green chiles
    • 28 oz tomato sauce
    • About 1 tbsp stone-ground mustard
    • 8 oz beef broth
    • 31 oz Bush's Chili Magic (about two 15.5 oz cans)
    • 2 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap

    Instructions

    1. Brown the chili-grind beef.
    2. Add the chili paste (or tomato paste), onion, jalapeños, Williams chili seasoning, and garlic.
    3. Pour in the beer and scrape up the browned bits on the bottom of the pot.
    4. Stir in the diced tomatoes, Hatch green chiles, tomato sauce, stone-ground mustard, and beef broth.
    5. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook 2 hours.
    6. Stir in the Chili Magic and Zippy Zap. Cover and simmer 1 hour more.

    Notes

    • The transcript sounds like 'chili paste' right after the meat browns; in Pete's other chilis this step is 6 oz tomato paste, so use that if chili paste is unclear.
    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.

    Chuck Wagon Chili

    Beef Chili · About 3 hours simmering, plus searing and a 15-minute roast

    Watch the video

    A from-scratch chuck wagon chili with seared cubes of chuck roast and a puree of oven-roasted garlic and peppers.

    Ingredients

    • 2 1/2 lb chuck roast, trimmed and cubed
    • A couple of tablespoons oil, for searing
    • 1 large yellow onion
    • 1 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap
    • Peppers, seeded (type and amount not specified)
    • 10 cloves garlic
    • A little oil, for roasting the garlic and peppers
    • 1 cup beef stock
    • 1/2 tbsp cumin
    • 4 cups garden-fresh tomatoes
    • 32 oz tomato sauce

    Instructions

    1. Heat a couple of tablespoons of oil and sear the cubed chuck in small batches, just to brown the outside. Set the meat aside.
    2. Add the onion and Zippy Zap to the pot and cook a few minutes, then return the meat.
    3. Seed the peppers. Toss the peppers and 10 cloves of garlic with a little oil and roast at 350°F for 15 minutes.
    4. Blend the roasted garlic and peppers with 1 cup beef stock until you have a chili-pepper puree, then add it to the pot.
    5. When the meat is cooked through, add the cumin, garden-fresh tomatoes, and tomato sauce.
    6. Cover and simmer on low 3 hours.

    Notes

    • Pete never names the peppers or says how many go into the roast, only that the seeds come out and they get pureed with the garlic.
    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; he says the link is in his bio. Use another hot sauce if you don't have it.

    Competition-Style Hot Dog Chili

    Beef Chili · Makes an 8-quart pot · About 2.5 to 3 hours

    Watch the video

    A smooth, competition-style chili with everything but the sirloin pureed, thickened with oatmeal and refried beans, and meant to be ladled over a hot dog.

    Ingredients

    • Splash of olive oil
    • 3 lb ground sirloin (93/7)
    • About 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 4 oz Hatch green chiles, pureed
    • 1 medium yellow onion, pureed
    • 2 fresh jalapeños, pureed
    • 20 oz fire-roasted Rotel, pureed
    • 2 tbsp fresh minced garlic
    • 6 oz tomato paste
    • 3 packets (1 oz each) Williams Original Chili Seasoning
    • 16 oz beef stock
    • 1 oz packet oatmeal
    • 15 oz refried beans (Pete prefers the packet over the can)
    • About 2 tbsp yellow mustard
    • About 2 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap

    Instructions

    1. In an 8-quart pot, add a splash of olive oil and the ground sirloin.
    2. Add the Worcestershire, pureed Hatch green chiles, onion, jalapeños, and Rotel, plus the garlic, tomato paste, Williams chili seasoning, beef stock, and oatmeal.
    3. Stir well and break the meat up. Cover and cook on low for 1 hour.
    4. Stir in the refried beans, yellow mustard, and Zippy Zap.
    5. Cover and cook on low 1.5 hours more, stirring about every 30 minutes.
    6. After 2.5 to 3 hours total, it should be thick enough to spoon over a hot dog.

    Notes

    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.
    • The oatmeal is a competition-chili thickener; use a 1 oz packet as stated, not a breakfast bowl of oats.

    Cook-Off Chili with Pete's Tweaks

    Beef Chili · Makes a 10-quart pot · About 1.5 hours, plus browning

    Watch the video

    The same cook-off chili with a few changes Pete says might help you take first and second place: fire-roasted Rotel, beef broth, Worcestershire, and a little yellow mustard.

    Ingredients

    • 3 lb ground beef
    • 1 lb hot breakfast sausage (Jimmy Dean Hot)
    • About 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 6 oz tomato paste
    • 1 large white onion, chopped
    • 2 fresh jalapeños, chopped, seeds and all
    • 3 packets (1 oz each) Williams Original Chili Seasoning
    • 3 cans (10 oz each) fire-roasted Rotel
    • 1 can (15 oz) petite diced tomatoes
    • 1 can (29 oz) tomato sauce
    • 1 can (15 oz) beef broth
    • 1 1/2 tbsp fresh minced garlic
    • 3 cans (15.5 oz each) Bush's Chili Magic, Campfire Style
    • 1 1/2 tbsp yellow mustard
    • Splash of Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap (optional, to finish)

    Instructions

    1. In a 10-quart pot, start browning the ground beef. While it cooks, add the Worcestershire sauce and tomato paste.
    2. Add the hot breakfast sausage, onion, jalapeños, and Williams chili seasoning. Cook until the meat is fully done.
    3. Stir in the fire-roasted Rotel, petite diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, beef broth, and minced garlic.
    4. Cover and simmer on medium-low heat for 1 hour.
    5. Stir in the Bush's Chili Magic and yellow mustard. Cover and simmer on low at least 30 minutes more.
    6. Finish with a splash of Zippy Zap if you like.

    Notes

    • Pete presents this as a couple of small changes to his winning cook-off chili.
    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.

    Crockpot Cook-Off Chili

    Beef Chili · Makes a 10-quart crockpot · 3 hours on high or 6 hours on low, plus browning

    Watch the video

    The award-winning cook-off chili adapted for the crockpot. Brown the meats first, then load everything into a 10-quart slow cooker.

    Ingredients

    • 3 lb ground beef (80/20)
    • 1 lb breakfast sausage
    • 6 oz tomato paste
    • 1 large onion, chopped
    • 2 large fresh jalapeños, chopped
    • 4 oz Hatch green chiles
    • 2 tbsp fresh minced garlic
    • 44 oz diced tomatoes
    • 44 oz tomato sauce
    • 3 oz Williams Original Chili Seasoning
    • 3 cans (15.5 oz each) Bush's Chili Magic, Campfire Style
    • Splash of Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap (optional, to finish)

    Instructions

    1. In a skillet, brown and drain the ground beef.
    2. Add the breakfast sausage and tomato paste. Cook until the sausage is fully done; do not drain.
    3. Transfer the meat to a 10-quart crockpot.
    4. Add the onion, jalapeños, Hatch green chiles, garlic, diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, Williams chili seasoning, and Bush's Chili Magic. Stir well.
    5. Cover and cook on high for 3 hours or on low for 6 hours.
    6. Finish with a splash of Zippy Zap before serving.

    Notes

    • This is the cook-off chili packed into a crockpot, with the Chili Magic added at the start instead of at the end.
    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.

    Hamburger Chili Dog Chili

    Beef Chili · About 2.5 hours, plus browning

    Watch the video

    A five-pound pot of hamburger chili with ketchup, mustard, and broth, simmered until it's ready for a chili dog. Pete also posted this same recipe as hamburger chili.

    Ingredients

    • 5 lb ground beef
    • 1 large yellow onion (part of the 'Texas Trinity')
    • 3 jalapeños
    • About 6 cloves minced garlic
    • About 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 1/3 cup chili powder
    • 1 tbsp onion powder
    • 1 tbsp garlic powder
    • 1 tbsp salt
    • Black pepper (amount not specified)
    • Cumin (amount not specified)
    • 1 bay leaf
    • About 2 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap
    • 6 oz tomato paste
    • 15 oz fire-roasted tomatoes
    • 10 oz Rotel
    • 32 oz beef broth
    • 2 tbsp mustard
    • About 1/4 cup ketchup

    Instructions

    1. Brown the ground beef, breaking it up well, then drain it. Pete wraps foil around the strainer so the grease doesn't go down the drain.
    2. Add the onion, jalapeños, garlic, Worcestershire, chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, cumin, bay leaf, Zippy Zap, and tomato paste. Mix well and cook about 10 minutes on medium heat.
    3. Stir in the fire-roasted tomatoes, Rotel, beef broth, mustard, and ketchup.
    4. Bring to a low simmer, cover, and cook 2.5 hours, stirring occasionally.

    Notes

    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.
    • Also shown in: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SXO77xGvt3M

    Pete's Favorite Chili

    Beef Chili · About 1 hour simmering, plus browning

    Watch the video

    The chili Pete calls his favorite: beef and pan sausage with Rotel, ranch-style beans, and a can of Chili Magic.

    Ingredients

    • 2 lb ground beef
    • 1 lb breakfast or pan sausage
    • 1 onion, chopped
    • 2 jalapeños, chopped
    • About 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 6 oz tomato paste
    • 30 oz Rotel
    • 1 tbsp fresh minced garlic
    • 2 packets (1 oz each) chili seasoning (your favorite)
    • 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
    • 1 can Bush's Chili Magic
    • 1 can (26 oz) ranch-style beans
    • About 2 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap
    • 24 oz beef broth

    Instructions

    1. Cook the ground beef and sausage until the meat is done all the way through, then strain the meat and set it aside.
    2. In the pot, cook the onion and jalapeños with the Worcestershire and tomato paste.
    3. Return the meat to the pot. Add the Rotel, garlic, chili seasoning, tomato sauce, Bush's Chili Magic, ranch-style beans, Zippy Zap, and beef broth.
    4. Leave it on and simmer 1 hour.

    Notes

    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.
    • He doesn't name a brand of chili seasoning in this video, just two 1-oz packets of your favorite.

    Watch the video

    A crockpot white chicken chili with a rotisserie garlic-herb chicken, white kidney beans, roasted Hatch chiles, and cream cheese.

    Ingredients

    • 1 fully cooked, deboned garlic-herb rotisserie chicken (Pete uses a grocery-store roasted chicken)
    • 1 large onion, chopped
    • 1 can (28 oz) fire-roasted tomatoes
    • 12 oz roasted Hatch green chiles
    • 3 cans (15.5 oz each) white kidney beans, rinsed
    • 1 tsp dried oregano
    • 1 tsp ground coriander
    • 1 tsp ground cumin
    • 1 tsp ground black pepper
    • 1 tbsp chili-lime adobo seasoning

    • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
    • 1 block (8 oz) cream cheese
    • 4 ears fresh corn, kernels cut off (or 1 can of corn)
    • About 1/2 cup fresh chopped cilantro
    • Juice of 1 whole lime
    • 1 packet (1.27 oz) chicken fajita seasoning
    • 48 oz chicken broth
    • Splash of hot sauce, to taste (Pete adds 'Heat Blast')

    Chili Cheese Tots Casserole

    Chili Mac & Casserole · 30 minutes in the oven, then 10 minutes more with cheese (oven temperature not specified)

    Watch the video

    A layered chili casserole with seasoned ground meat, beans, and cornbread mix poked down into the pot, then lined with tater tots and finished with cheese.

    Ingredients

    • 2 lb ground meat
    • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
    • 1 tsp cumin
    • 1 tbsp black pepper
    • 1 tbsp chili powder
    • 1 medium onion
    • 1 fresh jalapeño
    • About 4 cloves minced garlic
    • 1 can (10 oz) fire-roasted Rotel
    • 1 can (15 oz) beans (Pete uses pinto; use whatever you like)
    • 2 1/2 cups cornbread mix (Pete uses a unsweetened mix; use whatever you like)
    • Tater tots, enough to line the top (amount not specified)
    • Cheese, for topping (amount not specified)

    Instructions

    1. Cook the ground meat with the cayenne, cumin, black pepper, and chili powder.
    2. Add the onion, jalapeño, and garlic (the Texas Trinity), plus the fire-roasted Rotel. Cook a few minutes to soften the vegetables.
    3. Stir in the beans, then flatten everything out in the pan.
    4. Add the cornbread mix and poke it down into the chili with a fork so it works in throughout.
    5. Line tater tots over the top. Bake 30 minutes (oven temperature was not stated).
    6. Cover with cheese and return to the oven for 10 minutes.

    Notes

    • The oven temperature is not given in the video, only the 30-minute and 10-minute times.
    • The amount of tater tots and cheese is not specified; cover the top.

    Chili Mac & Cheese

    Chili Mac & Casserole · Until the pasta is cooked the way you like it

    Watch the video

    A one-pot chili mac: seasoned ground meat, crushed tomatoes, chili beans, and rotini that cook right in the sauce, finished with cheese.

    Ingredients

    • Splash of olive oil
    • 2 lb ground meat
    • 1 onion
    • 1 bell pepper
    • 1 fresh jalapeño
    • 1 tsp cumin
    • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
    • 2 tsp black pepper
    • 1 tsp garlic salt
    • 2 tsp smoked paprika
    • 2 tsp chili powder
    • 28 oz crushed tomatoes
    • 3 cups pasta (Pete uses rotini)

    Poor Man's Chili Mac

    Chili Mac & Casserole · About 30 minutes simmering, plus boiling the pasta

    Watch the video

    An old-school, tight-budget chili mac: a tomato-chili beef sauce simmered with sink water, then tossed with jumbo elbows and a little cheese.

    Ingredients

    • 2 lb ground beef
    • 3 oz tomato paste
    • 1 medium onion, chopped
    • 1 tbsp garlic powder
    • 1 tbsp chili powder
    • 1/2 tbsp cumin
    • Ground red pepper (amount not specified)
    • Smoked paprika (amount not specified)
    • 3 1/2 cups water (Pete calls it '117-degree clear sink water')
    • 1 can (14.5 oz) chili-style tomatoes
    • Salt, to taste
    • 12 oz jumbo elbow noodles
    • Splash of habanero sauce (optional)
    • Cheese, to taste (amount not specified)

    Instructions

    1. Brown the ground beef, breaking it up well. Stir in the tomato paste and let it cook with the beef.
    2. Add the onion, garlic powder, chili powder, cumin, ground red pepper, and smoked paprika.
    3. Stir in the water and chili-style tomatoes. Bring to a simmer and cook 30 minutes.
    4. Meanwhile, salt a pot of boiling water and cook the jumbo elbows 6 to 7 minutes. Drain.
    5. Taste the sauce and add salt as you like. Turn the heat off, add the noodles, and stir them in.
    6. Finish with a splash of habanero sauce and a little cheese if you want. Pete also likes it in a fold-over sandwich.

    Notes

    • Amounts for the ground red pepper and smoked paprika were not given separately from the 1/2 tbsp cumin.

    A baked white-chicken chili mac with a Hatch chile and Rotel cheese sauce, rotisserie chicken, and a Colby Jack topping.

    Ingredients

    • 1 stick unsalted butter
    • 1 medium onion, chopped
    • 1 fresh jalapeño, chopped
    • 4 oz Hatch green chiles
    • 10.5 oz fire-roasted Rotel
    • 1 tbsp fresh minced garlic
    • 1 tbsp chili powder
    • Black pepper (amount not specified)
    • Garlic powder (amount not specified)
    • About 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 32 oz white American cheese (from the grocery store deli)
    • 12 oz evaporated milk
    • 1 rotisserie chicken, deboned and chopped
    • 1 lb macaroni, boiled 8 minutes (al dente)

    Chili Cheese Dip

    Dip · Until the vegetables soften, plus about 10 minutes to melt the cheese

    Watch the video

    A party chili-cheese dip with seasoned beef, Hatch chiles, and a trio of cheeses melted with evaporated milk and cream cheese.

    Ingredients

    • 1 1/2 lb ground beef
    • 1 1/2 oz your favorite chili seasoning
    • 1 small onion, chopped
    • 2 fresh jalapeños, diced
    • 4 oz Hatch green chiles
    • 1 can (15 oz) petite diced tomatoes
    • About 1 tbsp ancho chili powder (or regular chili powder)
    • About 1 tbsp Pepperbellypete's Zippy Zap
    • 16 oz pepper jack cheese
    • 16 oz medium cheddar cheese
    • 16 oz sharp white cheddar cheese
    • 12 oz evaporated milk
    • 8 oz cream cheese

    Instructions

    1. Brown the ground beef with the chili seasoning, onion, jalapeños, Hatch green chiles, and petite diced tomatoes.
    2. Bring to a simmer on medium heat and cook until the vegetables soften.
    3. Stir in the ancho chili powder and Zippy Zap.
    4. Add the pepper jack, medium cheddar, sharp white cheddar, and evaporated milk. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and stir about every 5 minutes.
    5. After about 10 minutes, stir in the cream cheese and keep going until everything is incorporated.

    Notes

    • Zippy Zap is Pete's own hot sauce; use another hot sauce if you don't have it.
    • If you don't have ancho chili powder, Pete says regular chili powder is fine.
